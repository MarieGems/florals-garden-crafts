Questions about my plushies

How do I buy a plushie?

Tap Buy now under the plushie you want. That takes you to a secure Shopify checkout with that plushie already in your cart.

How many of each plushie are available?

Only 2 of each plushie are available, and what you see in the photos is what you get.

Do you make custom plushies?

No, I don’t take custom plushie orders. I’m in school, and school comes first. Every plushie in the shop is already made.

How much is shipping?

Shipping for plushies is a flat $7.50 per order, no matter how many plushies you buy. Right now plushies ship to US addresses only.

How big are the plushies?

The sitting bee is about 6.5 in tall and 6 in wide with the wings. The chunky axolotl is about 9.5 in tall sitting up and 7 in wide. The loaf cat is about 7 in wide and 10 in tall with the tail up. The triceratops is about 7.5 in long, 5.5 in wide, and 4.5 in tall. Each plushie has a photo next to a ruler, so you can see the size for yourself.

Who makes the plushies, and who runs the shop?

I’m Flora, and I crochet every plushie by hand. My mom runs the shop side: orders, payments, and shipping go through her Marie Gems Shopify store, so your checkout and card statement say “Marie Gems.”
