the maker

Hi, I'm Flora!

I started making plushies as a hobby to pass the time when I began homeschool. After a while, I wanted to make plushies for other people too — so now I'm using this website to share what I create.

Drawing came from my cousins — they got me into it, and now I do it just for fun. I wanted a place to share my art with everyone along with my plushies, so here it all is!
